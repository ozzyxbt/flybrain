How the game works: each throw is one 500 ms observation of the table by the network; the fixed DNp20 left/right readout sets where the ball lands (right − left, divided by a published scale). A hit sinks the cup and the next observation carries the Stonkfly reward pulse: a 20 mV-equivalent current into the 15 PAM11 dopamine cells for 200 ms, with the candidate plasticity rule enabled (measured PAM11 spikes are shown; this is engineered reinforcement, not pleasure). A miss makes the fly drink: each drink blurs and doubles the rendered image the network sees on later throws (see FLY CAM). No neural parameter is changed by drinking; the wobble is animation. Six drinks and the fly passes out.
A game. Not evidence of skill, learning, intoxication or experience. Spike arrays, frames and results are hash-chained and replayable.
